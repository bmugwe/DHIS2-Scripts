Instructions on how to complete Nutrition Service monthly summary

This reporting tool will be filed by the designated healthcare workers.

1) When to be filled:
At the end of every month and submitted before 5th of the following month.

2) On the cover page:
County: Indicate the County where the facility is located
Sub County: Indicate the Sub County where the facility is located
Facility: Indicate the Facility name
MFL Code: Indicate the master facility List Code for the facility
Service Delivery Point: Indicate where the services are offered
Start Date: Indicate the first date of use
End Date: Indicate the last date of use

3) To be filled monthly
County: Indicate the County where the facility is located
Sub County: Indicate the Sub County where the facility is located
Facility: Indicate the Facility name
Month/Year: Indicate the month and year for which the information is being reported.

4) Monthly Summary for Nutrition Services
Record the total number of patients/clients seen in that month as per the specific client age disaggregate and gender:
Client Category

Record the total number of clients as either New or Revisi

Nutrition Diagnosis

Record the total number of clients diagnosed with SAM, MAM, AT RISK, and OVERWEIGHT/OBESE

Nutrition Intervention

Record the number of clients receiving nutrition treatment
a) Therapeutic Food
b) Supplementary Food
c) Micronutrients

Treatment Outcome

Record the outcomes for all the clients enrolled in Nutrition treatment during the reporting period. The outcome includes Cured, Defaulted, Died and or Non-Recovered)

The information to be filled in the above-mentioned section will be obtained from the MOH 407 Register based on the instruction provided on the register summary.

N/B: The age band 18-19 years it is a subset of the 18+ years.

5) Report prepared by:
The person(s) responsible for the preparation of this report should write his/her full name, telephone contacts, designation, signature and date of signing
